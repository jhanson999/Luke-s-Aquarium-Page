Privacy Policy

How your information is handled when you use Luke’s Aquarium.

Effective date: November 16, 2026   ·   App: Luke’s Aquarium (Roku screensaver)

Luke’s Aquarium ("we," "our," or "the screensaver") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the standalone Luke’s Aquarium screensaver handles information, and distinguishes the screensaver from the Roku platform and this informational website.

1. Information collected by the screensaver

The current version of Luke’s Aquarium does not collect personal information from you. It does not request a name, email address, location, account credentials, or payment information. It does not contain advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or built-in network requests to transmit viewing or device information to the developer.

The animation uses artwork and resources packaged with the app. It does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its screensaver features.

2. Use, sharing, and sale of information

Because the screensaver does not collect personal information, we do not use, sell, or share personal information gathered through the screensaver. We do not use the screensaver to serve personalized advertisements or to track you across apps or websites.

3. Local operation and technical messages

The screensaver may emit technical status messages while running in a developer debugging environment. These messages are local diagnostics and are not sent to a developer-operated server by the screensaver. The screensaver does not maintain a developer-operated database of viewing history.

4. Roku and other third parties

Roku operates the device, streaming platform, software store, and system services. Roku may collect device, usage, or diagnostic information under Roku’s own policies. Luke’s Aquarium does not control Roku’s data practices.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary website requests and related technical information under GitHub’s privacy statement. The pages provided by the developer use no cookies, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, sign-in forms, or third-party embedded widgets.

5. Children’s privacy

Luke’s Aquarium is a general-audience animated screensaver and does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children or adults. If you believe a privacy concern requires attention, contact us using the information below.

6. Data retention and security

We do not maintain developer-operated records of personal information collected through the screensaver. Information you send voluntarily when contacting support (for example, an email message) will be used to respond to your request and retained only as reasonably necessary for that purpose, subject to applicable law and your email provider’s practices.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app, website, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised effective date. If a future version of the screensaver adds network services, analytics, or data collection, the policy should be updated before that version is released.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer of Luke’s Aquarium at lukesaquariumroku@gmail.com.
