A simple aquarium for your Roku.

Your own underwater escape

Luke’s Aquarium is an animated freshwater aquarium screensaver created for Roku devices. Enjoy a colorful underwater environment with lifelike fish, gentle water effects, flowing plants, and a friendly diver in a yellow suit and sunglasses who occasionally swims in to wave hello.

It is an animated scene—not a live video feed or a real aquarium—and is designed as a relaxing backdrop when your Roku is idle.

Meet the fish

The current aquarium features 12 animated fish across five kinds:

  • 2 goldfish
  • 2 angelfish
  • 5 guppies
  • 2 neon tetras
  • 1 dwarf gourami

What you'll see

🐠

Natural fish movement

Individual swimming styles, varied speeds, and animated three-dimensional-looking turns.

🫧

Two bubble streams

Gentle bubbles rise from the gravel on the left and right sides of the aquarium.

🌿

Underwater scenery

A driftwood arch, plants, stones, gravel, and softly moving underwater lighting.

👋

A surprise visitor

A cheerful diver with a yellow helmet and sunglasses makes occasional waving appearances.

Installing Luke’s Aquarium

Once the screensaver is available for your device in the Roku Streaming Store, install it through Roku and choose it from your device’s screensaver or theme settings. Availability and menu labels may vary by Roku model and software version.

This website provides information about the screensaver; it does not install software or collect account details.
